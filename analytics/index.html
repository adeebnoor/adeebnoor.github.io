How to read these numbers

Visitors are approximate browser counts, not identified people. A browser identifier expires after 30 days. Link clicks include project, external, contact, language and download clicks; these categories are already included in the total.

A download click or CV print request does not confirm that a file was saved. Opening another project website records the outgoing click; actions inside that website require its own analytics. Visitors who opt out, use privacy signals or block tracking are not included.
