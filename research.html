Research

From model performance to real-world consequences.

My research asks what conventional evaluation leaves invisible: allocation identity, knowledge completeness, implementation context and the difference between a technically correct score and a consequential decision.

RIDI

Allocation Identity in AI

RIDI examines whether systems with the same declared metrics can still allocate scarce opportunities to different identities, creating different downstream outcomes.

Open interactive demo →

AI & decision intelligence

The wider agenda is to move AI evaluation beyond aggregate performance toward the structure of action: who or what is selected, excluded, exposed or prioritized when capacity is finite.

Biomedical knowledge

Work on drug–drug interaction knowledge, pharmacovigilance and precision medicine studies how incomplete or heterogeneous knowledge changes what clinicians and computational systems can safely infer.

Education & institutional systems

Research and system-building around readiness, evidence, curriculum design, research merit and governance connect the same question to universities and public institutions.
