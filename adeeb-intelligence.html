Independent Research Initiative
AIADEEB INTELLIGENCE

Researching how intelligence becomes action.

Adeeb Intelligence is an independent research initiative led by Adeeb Noor. We study how intelligent systems turn evidence into consequential decisions — and build methods that make those decisions measurable, auditable and human-accountable.

Mission →Research thrusts →Systems →Work with us →
Mission

Make consequential intelligence measurable, auditable and accountable.

Our work starts where a model output becomes an allocation, clinical alert, engineering action or institutional decision. We focus on the gap between what a system scores and what it actually changes.

Reveal hidden differencesDevelop evaluation methods that expose action-level divergence hidden by aggregate performance.
Strengthen evidence integrityTrace what evidence a system used, what it missed and when that changes a decision.
Translate research into useTurn methods into reproducible tools, public systems, applied studies and responsibly protected IP.

How we work

Measure. Trace. Stress. Translate.

We use a simple research discipline across domains.

01Measure the consequential difference

Look beyond average performance to the items, evidence or people whose outcomes actually change.

02Trace the decision state

Preserve provenance, assumptions, dependencies and evidence so the result can be inspected.

03Stress the claim

Change the condition, boundary or dependency and observe whether the decision still holds.

04Translate the finding

Release the result as a paper, reproducible artifact, public system, institutional pilot or protected invention.
