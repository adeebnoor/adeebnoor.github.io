Start with a specific question, not a generic request

Best fit includes students in computing, data, AI, biomedical informatics or education technology who can engage with literature, data or software and explain their reasoning clearly.

Who changes?

Measure how model and ranking updates alter the people or items selected by a decision.

What does missing knowledge hide?

Study biomedical knowledge completeness and drug interactions before inference.

What does the category mean?

Workforce position design and AI-assisted classification with MIYAR.

What counts as readiness?

AI-enabled education, judgment and evidence of capability with iSCARB and IMAM.

How to approach

What to send in the first message

Include your program and stage, a 3–5 line initial question, the skills or tools you can use, and a link to prior work when available. Do not send patient data or confidential institutional material.

Admissions and funding

Formal admission, funding and supervision registration are handled only through university systems and policies; this site is not an admissions portal.
