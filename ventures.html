Workforce Decision Intelligence

Kinetic HR

Public prototype · Early-warning decision layer
PrototypeUpdated 2026

My role: product concept, decision logic and workforce-intelligence direction.

See workforce pressure before it becomes a staffing crisis.

Kinetic HR is a bilingual workforce decision-intelligence prototype designed to sit alongside existing HR systems. It surfaces capacity signals, workforce gaps and decision queues, then lets users explore staffing scenarios without writing back to source systems.

The current prototype also exposes data-quality, provenance and audit views so recommendations can be traced to their measurement basis rather than presented as opaque AI output.

Early warningSurface workforce pressure, capacity gaps and decision bottlenecks before escalation.
Scenario labCompare staffing responses and their operational effect without writing back to source systems.
AuditabilityKeep data quality, provenance and decision logic visible for institutional review.

How I build with partners

Not “innovation theater.” A translation path.

I prefer partnerships where the decision rights, evidence, product boundary and path to adoption are explicit from the beginning.

01 · DEFINE

Frame the consequential problem

What decision is being made, for whom, and what changes if the system is wrong?

02 · EVIDENCE

Make the knowledge testable

Identify data, assumptions, validation criteria and the evidence required for trust.

03 · SYSTEM

Build the operating layer

Translate the research into a workflow, product, API, model or institutional capability.

04 · SCALE

Design for adoption

Governance, partnership model, commercialization and implementation are part of the design.
