# Kinetic HR v0.6 — Pilot Readiness

## Added in this review-pilot layer
- Expert Reviewer Mode tied to exact state fingerprint.
- 10-dimension HR review scorecard.
- Methodology and metric definitions inside the product.
- Evidence explorer with gap episode timeline and incidence denominator.
- Evidence trail appended to operational detail drawer.
- Structured feedback capture and JSON/Markdown export.
- Pilot database blueprint with tenant isolation, versioned datasets, scenarios, alerts, append-only audit and reviewer feedback.

## Still intentionally not claimed as production-ready
- No live ERP connector.
- No server-side database connected in the public demo.
- No SSO/RBAC in the public demo.
- Public SSCO catalog remains a validated subset until the full official master is imported and governed.
- Service burden / scarcity / time-to-fill require calibration with each pilot entity.
- Scenario results are decision simulations, not guaranteed forecasts.

## Recommended reviewer flow
1. Open with `?review=1`.
2. Review Command Center and one Early Warning signal.
3. Open the signal drawer and inspect Evidence trail / Gap episode.
4. Run at least two intervention scenarios.
5. Review Methodology tab.
6. Complete 10-dimension review and structured feedback.
7. Export Markdown or JSON and send it back to the product team.
