# Kinetic HR — 30-minute HR Expert Review

1. **5 min — Concept**: Does the product solve a real workforce-planning problem that the ERP does not already solve well?
2. **7 min — Measurement**: Challenge Gap Episode, Prevalence, Incidence Rate, Velocity, Burden, Resolvability and Decision Priority.
3. **5 min — Evidence**: Open a signal and inspect its source row, event history, denominator, baseline and fingerprint.
4. **5 min — Scenario**: Compare Hire, Transfer, Upskill and Contract. Challenge lead times, success rates and donor spillover.
5. **3 min — Data feasibility**: Identify which required fields are unavailable or unreliable in real HRMS/ERP systems.
6. **5 min — Reviewer Mode**: Score the ten dimensions and export the review as Markdown or JSON.

## Questions we specifically want answered
- Would you trust this signal enough to trigger a management review?
- Is the definition of a Workforce Capacity Gap correct and operationally useful?
- What should define `required capacity` in your organization?
- Which service-impact inputs are available in practice?
- Who should own Kinetic HR internally: Workforce Planning, HR Strategy, OD, CHRO office, or another function?
- What would block adoption even if ERP integration were easy?
