A useful first message

Context beats a generic introduction.

To make the first conversation productive, include four things: the problem, the organization or research group, the decision you are trying to improve, and the kind of collaboration you have in mind.

01

The problem

What is happening now, and why is it consequential?

02

The context

Research group, organization, sector, users and constraints.

03

The decision

What decision, workflow or outcome needs to improve?

04

The engagement

Research, advisory, product, investment or another partnership model.
