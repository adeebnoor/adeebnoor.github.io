Three kinds of impact

Research. Institutions. Products.

The strongest work in my portfolio crosses boundaries: scholarship informs the system, the system exposes the next research question, and the institutional context determines whether either one survives.

01 · RESEARCH IMPACT

Make hidden decision structure visible.

From drug–drug interaction knowledge to allocation identity in AI, the research program focuses on variables that conventional evaluation can hide.

Examples: RIDI, D3 Pharmacovigilance, Anti-DDI, biomedical knowledge integrity.
Output: frameworks, datasets, software, experiments and publications.
