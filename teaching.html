Teaching

Teaching as systems engineering for human capability.

I design learning around readiness, evidence and authentic engineering decisions—not content coverage alone. The goal is to make students explain, build, test and defend their choices.

CPIT-455

Software Engineering II

Reliability, safety, security, resilience, reuse, distributed systems and systems of systems.

iSCARB

An AI-assisted lecture and curriculum system designed to transform source content into structured, evidence-rich engineering learning experiences with explicit readiness checks.

Teaching philosophy

Start with an ill-structured problem, establish the domain spine, teach the mechanisms, make misconceptions visible, and end with evidence that a student can act under realistic constraints.

Research-to-classroom

Research questions become teaching cases, and teaching problems become research questions. The boundary between scholarship and course design is deliberately porous.
