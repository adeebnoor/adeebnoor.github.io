Identity & sovereignty in education

IMAM: who owns the purpose of learning?

I connect the identity question in IMAM to who defines the purpose of learning, selects the knowledge and judges the evidence of readiness. I believe a university should be able to explain and revise those choices in its own language and disciplinary context, even when it uses external AI tools. I would judge that control by visible authority over curriculum, assessment and revision; an Arabic interface alone would not establish it.
