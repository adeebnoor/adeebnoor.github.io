What LEDD did

Make emergency-department status visible in real time.

The original pitch describes LEDD as a Live Emergency Department Dashboard implemented at King Abdulaziz University Hospital. It categorized patients using CTAS, displayed live case counts and estimated waiting time, and pulled data automatically from hospital systems for publication in the ER waiting room and on the web.

TRIAGE

CTAS-based visibility

Five triage categories were used, from resuscitation and major trauma to lower-acuity cases.

REAL-TIME FLOW

Live counts and waiting time

LEDD was designed to publish the number of cases in each triage category together with estimated waiting time.

PATIENT DECISION SUPPORT

Understand before arriving

The project aimed to help patients understand ER procedures and make a more informed decision before coming to the emergency department.

OPERATIONS

Support diversion and flow

The pitch proposed using visibility to redirect lower-acuity cases toward more appropriate care options and reduce complications and cost.

Network concept

Beyond one waiting-room screen.

The pitch positioned LEDD as a unified-network capability for patients, ER employees and healthcare-provider networks, and proposed linkage to channels such as 937 and an app. These are project propositions from the pitch, not claims of later deployment.
