01 / The vision

A national ambition, built through focused contributions.

Valuable knowledge lives in clinical protocols, diagnostic reasoning, hospital workflows and research. HEALTHx explores how to capture that knowledge, evaluate its readiness and develop responsible paths to implementation. The ambition is to grow a Saudi collaboration network spanning universities, healthcare organizations and industry.

National ambition describes the project’s intended reach; it does not denote a government mandate or an announced national program.

03 / Where you fit

One project. Five ways to move it forward.

Medical students are welcome alongside clinicians, researchers, engineers and innovation professionals. Each proposed contribution starts with a bounded question and a tangible output, agreed with the project lead.

04 / How we work

From a question to a responsible pilot.

Discovery research is active. Later stages are proposed workstreams, dependent on evidence, partners and the relevant approvals; no deployment timeline is claimed.

Ethics, privacy, clinical review and IP requirements are scoped for each contribution before work requiring those approvals begins.

05 / An invitation to contribute

Bring a question worth working on.

Tell us your field, the track that interests you, a problem you have observed and the time you can contribute. Students can propose a focused research question; organizations can propose a use case or a validation partnership.

Propose a contribution ↗Use the contact form

Expressions of interest are reviewed to agree scope, supervision, contribution credit and IP arrangements. Participation does not automatically confer a funded position or thesis supervision. Please send a non-confidential introduction; do not include patient information or unpublished invention details.
