HEALTHx

Digital Medical Innovation Platform

Transforming medical innovations and treatment protocols into globally marketable, licensable digital assets using artificial intelligence and instant digital delivery.

King Abdulaziz University Hospital · Jeddah, Saudi Arabia

What is HEALTHx?

HEALTHx is an integrated digital platform designed to convert medical innovations and treatment protocols developed within the university hospital into digital assets that can be marketed and licensed globally, supported by AI and instant electronic-payment workflows.
A smart medical-innovation commercialization model for a university hospital

The source concept positions HEALTHx as a uniquely integrated model for university-hospital medical innovation commercialization.

Core Services

🤖

AI for Medical Innovation Marketing

Summarizes medical innovations and treatment protocols in commercially compelling language, automatically translates content into global languages, and produces professional investor-facing profiles.

💊

Digital Health IP Translation

Converts treatment protocols, medical systems and diagnostic algorithms into digital products such as APIs, health applications and AI models while preserving the intellectual-property layer.

⚡

Instant Digital Licensing

Supports payment and immediate delivery of digital-health solutions through electronic-payment channels — from selection to delivery in minutes.

🩺

Health-as-a-Service

Offers specialized medical consulting, health-data analysis and tailored clinical services to hospitals and companies through subscription or project-based models.

Integrated business model: medical innovations are showcased → digital licensing enables instant payment → advisory services create longer-term contracts.

Security & Privacy

The platform concept is designed to market medical knowledge and innovation — not patient-identifying information. The source specifies anonymization, statistical/aggregate data use, access controls, encryption, monitoring and formal legal/medical review.

🔐 No Direct Patient Identifiers

No patient names, identity numbers, addresses, phone numbers or email addresses are intended for publication or use.

📊 Aggregate & Statistical Data

Success rates, study statistics, population-level analyses and group clinical metrics.

🏥 Innovation, Not Patient Data

The marketed assets are technologies, treatment protocols, algorithms and digital-health applications.

🛡 Technical Controls

The source proposes AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, MFA, role-based access control, access logging, 24/7 monitoring and secure deletion.

Compliance targets named in the source: HIPAA, GDPR, Saudi cybersecurity/health-data requirements, ISO 27001, ISO 27701, ISO 27018 and HL7 FHIR. These are presented as design/target requirements in the original concept.
