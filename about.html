The through-line

From understanding a problem to building the system around it.

My work has repeatedly crossed the boundary between analysis and implementation: biomedical informatics, hospital modernization, national workforce strategy, higher-education governance, AI evaluation, curriculum systems and research-to-market translation.

Professor · Researcher · Builder

I am a Professor at King Abdulaziz University working across decision intelligence, AI, digital health, software engineering, education and institutional transformation. My research asks what aggregate metrics hide; my operating work asks what institutions need to make better decisions at scale.

That combination matters because strong ideas often fail between publication and practice. I am most interested in that gap — where research has to become a workflow, policy, product, platform or operating model.

“Research matters most when it can survive contact with real decisions.”
